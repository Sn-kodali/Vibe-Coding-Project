## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** Oh, this is like a service marketplace but they're really leaning into we've got your back, the whole top of the page is basically saying dont worry, youre covered before you even see who's on there.
- **What confused them:** They couldn't tell if the guarantee is only for the new people or for everyone. Some providers have actual reviews and ratings, some don't, and they're all just tagged Verified the same way, so it wasn't obvious who actually needs the guarantee vs who's already proven themselves.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** That people don't book strangers because they're scared of getting burned, not because they don't have enough info about them. Like it's a what if this goes wrong fear, not a I don't know enough about this person fear.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** I built one profile page with the guarantee front and center, but what actually shipped ended up being the whole homepage with a list of providers. So the real moment I wanted to test, does someone actually feel okay clicking book on a total stranger, never really got tested. I only proved the message sounds reassuring, not that it changes anyone's mind at the actual decision point.

# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

I showed a high-fidelity prototype for Scenario 01: The Retention Engine. The prototype tests whether Growth PMs and customer teams can diagnose first-90-day churn by identifying delayed first value as the leading driver, reviewing supporting evidence, seeing affected accounts, and choosing a week-one activation intervention.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

My partner understood that the core hypothesis was: if the prototype clearly connects churn metrics, user evidence, affected accounts, and an intervention path, then users can decide what to test earlier instead of just seeing that churn is happening.

## Where they got confused / pushed back

They wanted the prototype to make the test clearer: the goal is not to prove 90-day churn reduction immediately, but to test whether delayed first value is the right driver to act on and whether a week-one activation intervention feels actionable.

## What I'll change as a result

I will make the hypothesis and kill switch more visible in the prototype by clearly labeling the leading driver, the affected accounts, and the intervention test. I will also reduce any language that makes the build feel like a finished product instead of a prototype testing one retention assumption.

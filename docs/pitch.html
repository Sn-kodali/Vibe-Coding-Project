Module 2 · The Frame

Problem & hypothesis

Problem

What was broken

B2B SaaS teams often know early churn is happening, but they struggle to connect the “why” to the next action. Funnel metrics show where users drop, customer notes show who is at risk, and user quotes explain frustration — but these signals usually live in separate tools. Churn Driver Discovery brings them into one AI-assisted workflow so Growth PMs can identify the leading first-90-day churn driver, review affected accounts, and choose the next intervention to test.

Hypothesis

The bet we tested

We believe Growth PMs will move faster from churn diagnosis to action if the product connects metrics, evidence, risky accounts, and intervention planning in one guided workflow. We’ll know we’re right when users can identify the top churn driver, understand the evidence behind it, and continue into an intervention workflow without needing separate analysis.

Module 6 · The Evidence

Metric & evidence

What the data showed

Live analytics showed 5 visitors, 7 page views, 1 view per visit, 1m 25s average duration, and 100% bounce rate. The strongest signal was that users reached the product but did not continue past the first screen. This showed initial interest, but also revealed an activation problem: the product explained the churn insight, but did not guide users strongly enough into the next action.

Module 6 · The Move

Data signal → the one fix

The surgical change

Signal: Users reached the AI Retention Dashboard, but 1 view per visit and 100% bounce showed they were not moving deeper into the workflow.

Fix (Branching): I added a clearer first-screen action path on Diagnose with a prominent “Start retention review” CTA and a 3-step workflow strip: Diagnose churn driver → Review affected accounts → Approve intervention.

Redeploy result: The updated version is live for the next test cycle. Success will be measured by lower bounce rate, higher views per visit, and more users reaching Affected Users or Intervention Plan.

The Call

Recommendation

ITERATE, keep refining
The evidence behind the call

Iterate. The data did not kill the product idea; it exposed the next product risk. Users reached the dashboard, which supports the value of the problem, but they did not continue into the core workflow. The right strategic move is to keep the product and improve activation depth by making the path from Diagnose to Affected Users to Intervention Plan impossible to miss.

The Human Layer

My story, friction · learning · aha

Across six modules

The biggest friction was realizing that a useful dashboard is not automatically a usable product. I built a workflow that could diagnose churn, show evidence, surface risky accounts, and plan an intervention, but the first live analytics showed users were still stopping at the first screen. My aha moment was that retention intelligence needs to guide action, not just present insight. That changed the final iteration from “make the dashboard richer” to “make the next step obvious.”
